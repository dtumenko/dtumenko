overview: Two photographers, one shared name, and a documentary eye for the unscripted moments that make a wedding day unforgettable.
challenge: Djokovic & Djordje were already well known and respected as wedding photographers, but their brand did not reflect that. They were using a generic PNG logo on their profiles, with no visual identity behind it. In a business built on taste and trust, couples judge a photographer by everything they see before the first meeting, and the brand was not living up to the quality of the work it represented.
story: I designed a new logo that captured the documentary, honest character of their photography, then built a full identity system around it with typography, colour and clear rules for how everything should look. The system was flexible enough to grow beyond the screen, into editorial layouts, printed pieces and eventually merchandise. Each application was designed to feel personal and crafted, the same way their photographs do.
results: 'The new logo caught on remarkably fast within their community and quickly became recognisable on its own. Their positioning took a visible step up: the brand finally matched the level of their work. In the period that followed, one of the photographers appeared on national television and won an award at a National Geographic photography competition. The identity has since grown into merchandise, a sign that it has become something people want to carry with them.'

Djokovic & Djordje is a documentary wedding photography duo based in Valjevo, Serbia, known for their natural, unobtrusive storytelling style. The brand identity redesign refreshed their existing logo and logotype, introduced a warmer, more cohesive color palette and typography system, and extended the new visual identity across print materials — including business cards and wedding albums. The goal was to give their brand the same honesty and warmth found in their photography, turning years of documentary work into an identity that feels just as personal as the stories they capture.
