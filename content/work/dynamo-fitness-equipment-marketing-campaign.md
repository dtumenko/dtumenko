overview: Brand marketing campaign for Dynamo Fitness Equipment. One cinematic world across a scroll-driven newsletter, email, motion ads and Instagram.
challenge: |-
  Black Friday is the loudest week of the retail year. Inboxes and feeds fill up with red, yellow, percentage signs and countdown timers, and fitness retail looks like everyone else: product cut-outs on white, starbursts, “doorbuster” banners.

  Dynamo had a strong offer, with up to $2,050 off home gym packages. But a home gym is a considered purchase, not an impulse buy. The campaign had to make a big number feel premium rather than cheap. It also had to hold together across email, web, paid social and the Instagram grid without turning into a set of unrelated discount banners.
story: |-
  The campaign goes the opposite way to the category: dark, patient and premium. The equipment is lit like a tech launch, alone in a black studio with one hard cyan rim light. AI-generated photography from Higgsfield, graded as a single shoot, sets the tone for every asset.

  The newsletter became a scroll-driven web experience, with callouts drawing onto the product and the saving counting up. The email, motion ads and Instagram grid share the same light, type and logo end card, so every touchpoint reads as one campaign.

The Weight Is Over is a Black Friday campaign for Dynamo Fitness Equipment, an Australian home gym retailer. The campaign covers a scroll-driven web newsletter, an HTML email, motion ads in story, feed, square and wide formats, and a nine-post Instagram grid, all built on one visual system.

All photography was generated in Higgsfield Soul Cinema from a single art-directed brief. It was then graded to one black point, one colour split and one grain, so the set reads as a single shoot. The newsletter runs on GSAP ScrollTrigger, and all motion was built in code.
